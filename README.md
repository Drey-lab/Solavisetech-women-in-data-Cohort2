# Solavisetech-women-in-data-Cohort2
Women in Data Cohort 2 — Solavisetech
## About the Program

The Solavisetech Women in Data program is a training initiative designed to 
equip women with practical data skills covering data analysis, visualization, 
and data-driven decision making. This cohort brings together passionate women 
who are ready to break into and grow within the data field

## About Me
Hi! My name is Audrey Karen Klenam Avorgbedor. I am passionate about using data to tell stories 
and solve real-world problems. I come from a background in Cybersecurity and I am excited to be part of this amazing cohort.

## My Expectations from the Program

- To gain hands-on, practical experience working with real datasets
- To connect and collaborate with like-minded women in tech
- To receive mentorship and guidance from industry professionals
- To build a portfolio of projects that showcase my data skills

- **Data Analysis** — How to clean, process, and interpret data

- **Storytelling with Data** — How to communicate insights effectively to stakeholders
